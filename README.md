## Why this list is different

Most opportunity lists are copied from other lists. This one is read off the source.

- **Every deadline carries a receipt.** Not "closes in October" — the organisation's own sentence, quoted verbatim in [`data/opportunities.json`](data/opportunities.json). For *Yuva Yodha Energy Tech Hackathon 2026*:
  > “Registration & Idea Submission Aug 15- Oct 4”
- **Only the organisation's own page.** Never Unstop, never a LinkedIn repost, never a Telegram forward. CI rejects aggregator hosts on every pull request.
- **Closed rows are deleted, not archived.** If it is on this list, it is open. A daily watcher re-reads ~45 sources and the board is rebuilt every Saturday.
- **We say who it is *not* for.** Every row names the range and its edge — "third year and up; first and second years cannot register" — so you find out in one line instead of at the end of a form.
- **Nothing is paid placement.** No organisation pays to be listed, which is why rows can say *skip this*.

## 🪤 Traps we caught

Deadlines that are not what they look like. Each one is quoted from the source.

- **GATE 2027 registration** — 5 Oct is the last day WITHOUT a late fee; the portal stays open with a late fee until 12 Oct. Their banner says a VERIFIED DigiLocker account is mandatory, but the same site allows registering with an unverified DigiLocker account and the default ID proof.<br><sub>[IIT Madras (organising institute)](https://gate2027.iitm.ac.in/) · closes 5 Oct 2026</sub>
- **Smart India Hackathon 2026** — The 5th is the portal's outer date, and it is a SPOC nomination, not a student signup. Your **college's internal round has almost certainly already closed**; if your team was not picked internally, this extension does not help you.<br><sub>[Ministry of Education Innovation Cell / AICTE](https://www.sih.gov.in/sih2026PS) · closes 5 Oct 2026</sub>
- **Chevening Scholarships 2027-28 (India)** — Closes **11:00 UTC — 4:30 PM IST**, not midnight. An evening of the 6th does not exist for this one.<br><sub>[UK FCDO](https://www.chevening.org/scholarship/india/) · closes 6 Oct 2026</sub>
- **Knight-Hennessy Scholars 2027** — Closes **1 PM Pacific = 01:30 IST on 7 Oct**, and Stanford’s own programme deadline is a separate application in December. The scholarship form is not the admission.<br><sub>[Stanford University](https://knight-hennessy.stanford.edu/admission) · closes 6 Oct 2026</sub>
- **KPMG Capture the Flag 2026 — Campus edition** — Registration closes 6 October and the CTF starts 9 October — **final-years only**, and KPMG says outright that participation 'does not guarantee a job offer'.<br><sub>[KPMG in India](https://primary.researchsurvey.co.in/s/hackathon-capture-the-flag-2026/tt-XH79V) · closes 6 Oct 2026</sub>
- **ISRO LPSC Scientist/Engineer 'SC' — LPSC/03/2026** — Closes at 2 pm on 13 Oct, not midnight. Qualification must be complete by 13 Oct 2026 — final-year M.Tech students are not eligible.<br><sub>[ISRO Liquid Propulsion Systems Centre (LPSC), Thiruvananthapuram](https://www.lpsc.gov.in/) · closes 13 Oct 2026</sub>
- **Hacker House Goa 2026** — The application window was **extended to 20 October** — but the residency itself runs 28–31 October, so if picked you have about a week to book travel.<br><sub>[247 PM Studio](https://hacker-house-goa-2026.devfolio.co/) · closes 20 Oct 2026</sub>
- **Commonwealth Master's Scholarships 2027-28** — The CSC date is 16:00 BST on 20 Oct, but India’s **Ministry of Education sets its own earlier internal date** — the CSC deadline is not your deadline.<br><sub>[Commonwealth Scholarship Commission (UK)](https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/apply/) · closes 20 Oct 2026</sub>
- **Kaggle: ARC Prize 2026 (ARC-AGI-2)** — The $275,000 Grand Prize is **judged on your solution writeup**, which must be open-sourced and attached within seven days of the 2 November submission deadline — leaderboard rank alone does not win it.<br><sub>[ARC Prize Foundation / Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/arc-prize-2026-arc-agi-2) · closes 26 Oct 2026</sub>
- **Solar Decathlon India 2026-27** — Both cheaper tiers are **sold out** and the final INR 7,500 tier is marked 'Opening soon' — only 100 slots remain, so 13 November is the outer date, not the real one.<br><sub>[IIHS + AEEE](https://sdichallenge.in/) · closes 13 Nov 2026</sub>
- **Kaggle: Google — The Gemma 4 Developer Agent Competition** — Two deadlines that look like one: the **paper track closes 12 November**, two weeks before the 25 November entry deadline for the main competition.<br><sub>[Google / Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/gemma-4-developer-agent) · closes 26 Nov 2026</sub>
- **Hacktoberfest 2026** — Pull requests **no longer count** this year. Swag comes from collecting stickers at Fests, livestreams and DEV challenges — and is mailed 8–12 weeks after October ends.<br><sub>[MLH + DEV + DigitalOcean](https://hacktoberfest.com/) · closes NaN undefined NaN</sub>

## How it stays true

```
daily 09:00 IST   watcher reads ~45 org pages and job boards
                  → a change only counts when the new line reads like a deadline
                  → opens an issue, and a draft row with the quote pre-filled
every Saturday    every link re-probed, expired rows deleted, new rows verified
                  on the org's own page, then the board and this file rebuild
on every PR       CI checks links resolve, rejects aggregator hosts,
                  and fails any open row missing its receipt
```

## Contribute

Know a deadline that is not here? **[Submit it in two minutes](../../issues/new?template=submit-opportunity.yml)** — we need the organisation's own link and the sentence on their page that states the date. Or [open a PR](CONTRIBUTING.md) against `data/opportunities.json`.

We would rather miss a posting than publish a wrong one. Corrections are welcome and land fast.

### The rules every row is held to

1. **Filter on the closing date, never the posting date.**
2. **Source only the organisation's own page.** Aggregator links are rejected by CI.
3. **Real money or real prestige.** No no-name "leadership programmes".
4. **Say when something is closed, dormant, or not open to Indian students** rather than reprinting last year's deadline.
